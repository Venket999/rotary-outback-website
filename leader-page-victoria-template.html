Victoria Porter: Turning Justice Into Action in Kenya

Paul Harris Fellow | Rotary Leader in Gender Justice & Maternal Health

Leading transformative programs that address gender-based violence, maternal health, and youth empowerment across Kenya.

Support Victoria's Work

Victoria Porter didn't set out to become one of the most influential Rotarians in gender justice and maternal health. But when she transferred from the Rotary Passport Club of Melbourne to the Rotary E-Club of Outback Australia, something shifted. As Youth Director, then Secretary, and finally President for three consecutive years, Victoria built something rare: a global network united around a single vision—equality and dignity for women and girls in Kenya.

In 2019, at Greg Marlow's District Conference in Alice Springs, Victoria earned a Paul Harris Fellowship for her groundbreaking work in gender-based violence prevention. A year later, she received an Avenue of Service award—recognition that her impact stretched beyond one club, into the very fabric of District 9560.

"The work in Kenya taught me something essential," Victoria reflects. "You can't separate health from justice, or empowerment from safety. Change requires all three."

Today, her programs touch four critical areas: prison dignity, maternal health, leadership for vulnerable youth, and education around consent and conflict resolution. Together, they've reached thousands of lives.

Building Safe Spaces, Healthy Mothers, Empowered Girls

🔒 Kitengela Prison Toilet Block Initiative

📍 Kenya 👥 Cairns Trinity & Mission Beach Rotary
The Problem: Women in Kenyan prisons often lack basic sanitation, creating health crises, undermining dignity, and perpetuating cycles of harm.
What We Built: Modern toilet blocks with clean water access, designed with input from incarcerated women.
Why It Matters: Dignity is the first step toward rehabilitation. When people have access to clean, private facilities, it signals that their humanity is seen and valued.
Support Prison Dignity Programs

👶 Kajiado Maternity Ward Medical Support

📍 Kenya 💰 District 9560 Grant
The Challenge: Rural maternity wards lack basic equipment for safe childbirth. Mothers deliver without sterile instruments, blood pressure monitors, or trained attendants—putting both mother and child at risk.
Our Response: We donated medical equipment AND funded intensive training for maternity staff. Real equipment. Real skills. Real lives saved.
Current Impact: Maternal mortality prevented through emergency preparedness. Newborns delivered safely.

⚽ Youth Leadership & Empowerment Program

📍 Nairobi Slums 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Youth mentorship
3,400+ young men from Nairobi slums have participated in soccer and mentorship programs. Simultaneously, 100+ youth have attended leadership camps—learning resilience, decision-making, and self-worth.
The Model: Sports isn't just recreation. It's a pathway to mentorship. Every match is followed by group discussion around life skills, conflict resolution, and future planning.
Parallel Track: "No Means No" Education Over 3,447 women and girls have been educated in sexual health, consent, and safe conflict resolution. Practical, culturally-sensitive education delivered by trained facilitators.

🏠 Jifunze International House

📍 Kenya 🤝 Rotary E-Club One & Darwin Nomads
A Safe Haven for Street Youth: When street children need shelter, safety, and a path forward, Jifunze House is there. This facility provides safe housing, education access, healthcare, and mentorship toward independence.
